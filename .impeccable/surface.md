# Rental workspace

Mode: Operate. User confirmed both landlords and renters. Existing Next.js starter; Tailwind 4 with PostCSS. UI-only with synthetic data and session state.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A property register with the confidence and discipline of a Swiss industrial manual.

OWN-WORLD: Off-white paper, carbon typography, visible hairline compartments, sharp corners, one hazard-red accent. Architectural photography provides context.

STORY: Landlords scan collection health and act on properties or maintenance; renters switch to Explore, save homes, and request sample visits.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Fixed left navigation, an oversized two-line title, a four-column portfolio strip, then three homes beside an activity rail. Add property sits beside the title. Mobile collapses to a two-column strip and one-column homes.

FORM: User-pinned Swiss Industrial Print; seed b53272bf. Signature interaction is the Manage/Explore workspace switch. Motion is limited to property image hover and notices.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
